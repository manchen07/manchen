Publications and Preprints

My research focuses on developing and evaluating statistical methods to address methodological challenges that arise when applying meta-analytic techniques to real-world datasets, including dependent effect sizes, selective reporting and publication bias, and design-comparable effect sizes. I am also interested in statistical methods for single-case experimental designs.
